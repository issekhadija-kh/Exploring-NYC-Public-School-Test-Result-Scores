# Exploring-NYC-Public-School-Test-Result-Scores

This study analyzes SAT performance data from new-york city public schools to identify top performing in stitutions and borough-level trends.

## Objective
To perform exploratory data analysis on NYC high school data to answer three questions:
   1. The best math results are at least 80% of the *maximum possible score of 800* for math, which NYC school are best for math?
   2. Which `'borough'` has the highest standard deviation in combined SAT (`'total_SAT'` being the sum of math, reading, and writing scores) scores?
